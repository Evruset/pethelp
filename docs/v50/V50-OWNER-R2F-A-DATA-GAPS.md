# OWNER V50 R2F-A data gaps

The specialist-selection surface renders only the default-off Owner specialist projection. The canonical V50 doctor-select prototype also depicts facts that are not present in that safe projection:

- portrait/photo;
- rating and review count;
- experience or tenure;
- biography and recommendation copy;
- languages, animal affinities, credentials, and document-verification status;
- a public doctor-profile destination.

`V50_DATA_GAP=YES` for those elements. They are omitted rather than inferred or synthesized. The neutral initial avatar is presentation derived from the authorized public doctor name and is not a clinic-identity asset.

`SERVICE_TAXONOMY_AUTHORITY_GAP=YES` remains unresolved. R2F-A uses the authoritative clinic-service UUID already carried by the booking journey; it does not infer a taxonomy or specialty hierarchy.

`DECISION_COMPARISON_BLOCKED_BY_DATA_AUTHORITY=YES` remains unchanged. No comparison, rating, ranking, or recommendation behavior is included.
